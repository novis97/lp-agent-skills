# Human Feel Reference — Making the Page Not Sound (or Look) AI-Generated

Visitors can't articulate why a page feels fake, but they bounce anyway. "AI-feel" is really two separate smells: generic copy voice and templated design. Both are fixable with deliberate choices. Apply this file during Phase 2 (copy) and Phase 4 (design), and run the audit in section 4 before delivery.

## 1. Copy: kill the AI voice

### Banned patterns (Indonesian and English equivalents)

These constructions are statistically over-represented in AI output. One instance is fine; a page full of them screams generated:

- **Grand-opening throat-clearing**: "Di era digital yang serba cepat...", "Dalam dunia bisnis yang kompetitif...", "In today's fast-paced world...". Start with the visitor's actual situation instead: "Stok di gudang bilang 40, di rak cuma 12."
- **The "bukan hanya... tapi juga" reflex** and its siblings: "bukan sekadar X, melainkan Y", "not just X, but Y". Once per page maximum.
- **Tricolon addiction**: "Cepat, mudah, dan terpercaya." Three parallel adjectives in every heading is a fingerprint. Vary rhythm — sometimes one word, sometimes a full sentence.
- **Empty power verbs**: wujudkan, tingkatkan, optimalkan, maksimalkan, hadirkan / unlock, elevate, empower, seamless, transform. Each of these can almost always be replaced by what literally happens: "wujudkan efisiensi" → "tutup buku bulanan dalam 2 jam, bukan 2 hari".
- **"Kami memahami bahwa..." / "We understand that..."** — show understanding through specifics instead of announcing it.
- **Perfectly parallel section headings** ("Mengapa Memilih Kami / Apa Yang Kami Tawarkan / Bagaimana Kami Bekerja"). Real businesses write uneven, opinionated headings.
- **Emoji as bullet decoration** (🚀✨💡✅) — instant template smell in 2026.
- **Hedge-everything language**: "dapat membantu", "berpotensi meningkatkan". Landing pages make claims. If a claim is true, state it; if it needs a caveat, give the caveat concretely.
- **Rhetorical-question openers stacked in a row** ("Lelah dengan X? Bingung dengan Y? Ingin Z?"). One is a hook; three is a pattern.

### What to write instead

- **Steal the customer's language.** The best source of human-sounding copy is real humans: ask the user for actual customer chats, reviews, complaint tickets. "Barangnya suka selisih pas stock opname" beats anything invented. If no material exists, write the way that customer would text a friend.
- **One opinion per page.** Generic pages have no point of view. A line like "Kami nggak percaya kontrak 12 bulan — kalau produknya bagus, orang bertahan sendiri" is unfakeable and memorable. Ask the user what they believe that competitors don't.
- **Concrete over abstract, always.** Name real objects, places, times, amounts: "laporan yang biasanya jadi jam 11 malam", "kirim dari gudang Cikarang sebelum jam 3". Specificity is the single strongest anti-AI signal because AI defaults to abstraction.
- **Match register to audience, then commit.** A page for warung owners can say "nggak ribet"; a page for CFOs can't. Pick one register (formal Anda vs casual kamu/lo) and never mix.
- **Vary sentence length on purpose.** AI produces medium-length sentences forever. Follow a long sentence with a short one. Like this.
- **Small asymmetries are trust signals**: a heading that's a fragment, a testimonial left slightly ungrammatical (real quotes are messy), a price ending in an odd number, a founder's note in first person singular ("Saya mulai bisnis ini karena...").
- **The read-aloud test**: read every section out loud. Anything you'd be embarrassed to say to a customer's face gets rewritten.

## 2. Design: escape the template look

The AI-template smell in 2026: centered hero + gradient blob background, Inter/Poppins font, three rounded cards with line icons, purple-to-blue accents, glassmorphism panels, illustration-style 3D characters. Any one element is fine; the full set is instantly recognizable. Counters:

- **If the page is being built through `landing-page-design-copy`**, its Vibe Spec takes precedence over the quick notes here.
- **Typography carries identity.** Choose one characterful display face for headings (serif, slab, or a grotesque with personality) paired with a plain body face — instead of one geometric sans for everything. Oversized type with tight leading reads "designed"; medium-size centered type reads "generated".
- **Commit to a palette with an opinion**: one dominant color drawn from the brand/product world (a warung page can be kunyit-yellow and charcoal; a legal service can be ink and cream), not the default blue/purple gradient. Limit to 2-3 colors used with conviction.
- **Break the grid somewhere.** One overlapping element, an off-center hero, a section where the image bleeds off-screen, handwritten-style annotation on a screenshot. Templates are perfectly aligned; designed pages have one deliberate violation.
- **Real imagery beats stock-anything.** Actual product photos, real team, real workspace — even slightly imperfect phone photos outperform polished stock and AI illustration for trust. If the user has none, prefer honest abstract/typographic sections over fake-people stock.
- **Icons: fewer, or none.** If every feature card gets a line icon, delete the icons and let typography do the work, or use numbers instead.
- **Details that feel authored**: custom section dividers instead of straight lines, a marquee of real client names in plain text, footnote-style asides, a visible "Diperbarui Juli 2026" in the corner. Texture = humanity.

## 3. Process: extract voice from the user

Add these to the Phase 1 interview when the user cares about feel (and default to asking at least the first one):

1. "Tulis 2-3 kalimat menjelaskan produkmu ke teman — persis seperti kamu ngomong, jangan dirapikan." → this becomes the voice reference for the whole page.
2. "Ada kalimat asli dari pelanggan (review, chat WA) yang bisa dipakai?" → raw quotes for testimonials and pain-point copy.
3. "Apa satu hal yang kamu percaya tapi kompetitormu nggak?" → the page's opinion.
4. "Sebut satu brand yang tone-nya kamu suka dan satu yang kamu benci." → register calibration.

## 4. De-AI audit (run before delivery)

- [ ] Zero instances of: "di era digital", "solusi terpadu/terbaik untuk kebutuhan Anda", "wujudkan impian", or equivalent throat-clearing openers
- [ ] "Bukan hanya... tapi juga" and tricolon headings: max one each on the whole page
- [ ] Every abstract benefit rewritten as a concrete scenario with a real object/number/time
- [ ] At least one opinionated line a competitor wouldn't dare copy
- [ ] Register consistent (Anda XOR kamu) and matched to audience
- [ ] Sentence lengths visibly varied; at least a few sub-5-word sentences
- [ ] No emoji bullets; no icon-on-every-card unless each icon earns its place
- [ ] Typography: heading face has character; not Inter/Poppins-for-everything
- [ ] Palette is brand-derived, not default blue/purple gradient
- [ ] At least one deliberate grid break or authored detail
- [ ] Read-aloud test passed on hero, problem section, and final CTA
- [ ] Testimonials sound like real speech (contractions, mild mess), not composed prose
